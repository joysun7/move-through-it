Meet Alicia

Encouragement with heart and real-life experience.

I earned a Bachelor’s degree in Social Work from Augustana College in 1985. My people-centered background also includes healthcare, decades of hands-on client work, and EFT/tapping and empowerment coaching, which I began exploring in 2001. I came to tapping through my own personal journey, then felt inspired to share the technique with others.

I believe people deserve to be met with warmth, respect, curiosity, and encouragement—not judgment. Sessions can be in person in Honolulu or online, depending on what works best for you. We’ll start with what’s happening in your life and what you’d like to feel, understand, or change.

Coaching is supportive and goal-oriented; it is not psychotherapy, medical care, or a promise of a particular result.

“You already have strengths. Let’s find a way to put them to work for you.”

What a first conversation can look like

Start simply.

  • Tell me what’s on your mind and what you’d like help with.
  • We’ll identify one or two priorities that matter to you.
  • We’ll discuss whether coaching feels like a good fit and what a next step could be.

No pressure to have all the answers before we talk.
