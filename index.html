Meet Alicia Tompkins

Intuition, experience & empowerment.

My work is rooted in a genuine interest in people and the experiences that shape us. I earned a Bachelor’s degree in Social Work from Augustana College in 1985, and my people-centered background includes healthcare, decades of hands-on client work, and empowerment coaching and EFT tapping, which I began exploring in 2001.

Over the years, I’ve developed a strong intuitive sensitivity to shifts in energy and to the nuances in people’s words, tone, and emotions. Sometimes I recognize a pattern that hasn’t yet been put into words; other times, a thoughtful question can open a door and help someone explore where a feeling or belief may have begun.

I bring warmth, deep listening, and practical experience to each session. Together, we can explore what’s beneath the surface, reconnect with your strengths, and identify a next step that feels right for you. Sessions are available in person in Honolulu or by phone for clients elsewhere.

Empowerment coaching and tapping-informed support are not a substitute for psychotherapy, medical care, or crisis support. Results and individual experiences vary.
